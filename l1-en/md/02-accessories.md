<a id="electrode"></a>

## Charging Contacts

The charging contacts attach to the robot and mate with the dock contacts to charge the battery.

Genisom L1 EDU and Genisom L1-W EDU include charging contacts as standard. Before using the charging dock with Genisom L1 or Genisom L1-W, confirm the contacts are installed.
